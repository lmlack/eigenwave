From linear algebra to the Fourier transform

See how the Fourier transform works

A signal is a list of numbers, so it's a vector. The Fourier transform writes that vector in a different basis, one made of waves. These pages build up to that, one idea at a time, and every page has something to drag, slide or play.

Start with vectors

The order follows Gilbert Strang's Introduction to Linear Algebra and Oppenheim and Willsky's Signals & Systems, with ideas from Steve Brunton's Fourier analysis lectures. The explanations and figures here are this site's own. Full references.
